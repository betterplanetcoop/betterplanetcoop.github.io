About BetterPlanet Co-op

BetterPlanet Inc. was founded in 2000 by Nicole and friends with a simple mission: create digital products that make the world better. We started experimenting with how the exponential reciprocity power of the internet could solve world problems.

Over time, we learned that the technology piece was only half the story. The other half was Advertising, Marketing, and Brand — the Agency side. We realized something powerful: What if we mixed these powers to actually make the planet better instead of tricking people to buy stuff?

What if we use the same sentiments that grab attention and direct the engagement to outcomes that make the planet better? That became our north star.

In 2023, BetterPlanet Co-op was founded as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit — a moment to take actionable next steps to create the planet we want, as a collaborative creative force. Every video we curate, every tool we build, and every partnership we forge is aligned with our 11 core pillars, designed to make real positive impact.
